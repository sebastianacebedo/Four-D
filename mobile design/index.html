Our Story
FOUR-D was born in the streets of Metro Manila — a vision to bring world-class furniture craftsmanship to Filipino homes. Founded by a family of artisans, we believe that great furniture is not just functional; it's a statement of identity and a legacy for the next generation. Every nail, joint, and finish is a testament to our dedication to the craft.
Our Journey
2012
Founded in Quezon City with a small workshop and a big dream.
2015
Expanded to Makati showroom. First commercial project in BGC.
2018
Launched custom bespoke line. Partnered with local interior designers.
2021
Opened flagship showroom in Pasig. 300+ pieces delivered Metro-wide.
2024
Launched mobile-first experience. Serving all 16 cities of Metro Manila.
